## SHORT DESCRIPTION
LivePulse treats cities as living organisms with heartbeats, moods, and voices. Compare any two cities through emotional weather, animated heartbeats, day/night cycles, and an AI Oracle that answers anything — powered by real-time data across 40+ global cities.

## FIRST COMMENT (Post this immediately after launch)

Hey Product Hunt! 👋 I'm Tahseen, and I built LivePulse in a pretty unusual way — entirely in conversation with Claude AI.

**The problem:** Every city comparison tool gives you spreadsheets. Temperature. Rent. Safety score. All useful. All lifeless. None of them answer the question that actually matters: *what does this city FEEL like right now?*

**The solution:** LivePulse doesn't show you data tables. It shows you:
- 🫀 Animated heartbeats (Dhaka pulses at 92 BPM, Toronto at 64)
- 🌊 Emotional weather (Is the city "Electrified" or "Contemplative"?)
- 🔮 An AI Oracle you can ask anything about any two cities
- ⏳ A Time Bridge showing both cities' position in their day

It covers 40+ cities from Dhaka to Tokyo to Lagos to São Paulo.

**The tech:** React, zero dependencies beyond React itself, Anthropic Claude API for the Oracle, deployed on Vercel.

**What's free:** 2-city comparison, heartbeats, emotional weather, 5 Oracle queries/day
**What's Pro ($5/mo):** Multi-city, cost of living, air quality, livability radar, unlimited Oracle

I'd love your honest feedback. What cities should I add? What features are missing? What's broken?

This is v3.0 — built from scratch and shipped in under 24 hours. Let me know what you think. 🙏
